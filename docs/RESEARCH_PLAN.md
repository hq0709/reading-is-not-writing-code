# Research plan

## Question and registered claim

Do medical vision-language models use the clinical information that can be decoded from their internal representations?

Primary falsifiable claim: under a capacity-controlled, patient-split evaluation, robust linear decodability at a consumed visual locus does not by itself establish direction-specific causal influence under probe-normal interventions.

Falsification: the claim is rejected if prospectively registered cells with positive controlled-decoding intervals also show concept-direction effects that exceed the same-alpha random and sham controls and the uncertainty bounds of every fixed unrelated clinical direction, replicated across the registered architecture/concept contrasts. A true negative is retained as `OBSERVED`; metrics, doses, loci, control identities, or direction-specificity rules are not changed after seeing results to rescue the headline.

## Method identity and forbidden rescues

The method identity is fixed: patient-level splits; the same capacity-matched linear readout across loci; repeated type-to-random-label controls; trained-versus-random initialisation floors; explicit nuisance probes; bidirectional, equal-norm intervention controls; preregistered dose scale; bootstrap uncertainty; and immutable run evidence. Forbidden rescues include replacing AUROC/selectivity after inspection, removing failed cells, changing prompts only for unfavourable concepts without a registered sensitivity analysis, adding a stronger decoder, changing the intervention vector family, or relabelling invalid runs as scientific negatives.

## Current evidence boundary

The repository already contains released observations for controlled decodability, utilisation, planted-ground-truth tests, direction mismatch, and an ISIC second modality. These remain governed by `docs/02_RESULTS.md` and `docs/10_RESULTS.md`. They are not reclassified here. `docs/10_RESULTS.md` also records invalid or incomplete intervention evidence, including an off-forward-path LLaVA `vis.last` hook, incorrect dose scaling, missing repeated control draws, and absent bootstrap intervals.

## ICLR 6+ causal-ownership gate

The current question is whether named clinical probe directions own the behaviour of their matching questions or act as causal aliases for a shared output-sensitive channel. The registered gate is `qwen7b-vislast-causal-ownership`. It reuses the accepted Qwen model snapshot, prompt-independent `vis.last` activations, six-direction probe bundle, hook evidence, patient split, and `reltoken` scale from immutable run `20260903T000321Z-caaae3ef346d-qwen-full`. It also treats the accepted 400-patient direction-specificity run `20260903T103508Z-456c81bad460-7a3edc4b` as discovery evidence rather than confirmatory matrix evidence.

The gate crosses the six fixed directions `Effusion`, `Atelectasis`, `Pneumothorax`, `Cardiomegaly`, `Mass`, and `Nodule` with six matching yes/no clinical questions. Model, revision, final visual block, direction bundle, alpha `+0.25`, 119 random directions, coordinate-permutation sham, seed 0, prompts, and question order are fixed before the new cohort is evaluated. Each question has one common unsteered baseline and all 126 nonzero interventions, yielding a 6 by 6 clinical effect matrix plus random and sham controls.

The evaluation cohort contains exactly 400 test rows from 400 patients absent from both earlier Qwen intervention cohorts. Selection reads no labels or images: exclude every patient represented in the accepted 200-row source cohort and 400-row direction-specificity cohort; choose one row per remaining patient by SHA-256 of `qwen-causal-ownership-row-v1\0<row_id>`; rank patients by SHA-256 of `qwen-causal-ownership-patient-v1\0<patient_id>`; and take the first 400. The canonical JSON row-ID list has SHA-256 `e657a9bd1707f13695b02a341047af6f53981295b840376eb0ea5bbebfe2d312`.

For question `c`, the clinical ownership statistic is

`O_c = Delta(c -> c) - max_{d != c} Delta(d -> c)`,

where `Delta(d -> c)` is the paired mean change in `P(yes)` caused by direction `d`. Uncertainty uses 5,000 joint patient bootstrap draws with seed `20260904`. The formal ownership test is implemented as all 30 fixed pairwise differences `Delta(c -> c) - Delta(d -> c)`: a studentized centered max-T critical value provides simultaneous 95% lower bounds over all 30 comparisons. A concept is owned when all five simultaneous bounds for its column are positive, its diagonal effect is positive, exceeds every one of the 119 same-column random directions, and exceeds the absolute sham effect. Beating all 119 random directions gives the minimum exact one-sided rank p-value `1/120`; Bonferroni over six columns controls this random-control family at 0.05.

For each fixed direction, the shared-alias analysis measures both its mean off-diagonal excess over the other five named diagonals and its mean absolute off-diagonal effect. A studentized centered max-T critical value provides simultaneous 95% lower bounds over all 12 statistics. A shared causal alias requires both bounds to be positive, and its absolute off-diagonal effect must exceed the global maximum across all registered random-direction summaries and the global maximum absolute sham effect. Direction selection occurs only among directions meeting that conjunction. The full matrix, all simultaneous bounds, random/sham comparisons, and bootstrap draws are reported regardless of disposition; valid outcomes are `OBSERVED` and implementation or measurement invalidity is `FAILED`.

The matrix decides the next mechanism gate without another route-selection round. A detected shared alias has first priority and locks the largest raw off-diagonal effect in its confirmed direction for output-Jacobian alignment, answer-encoding replacement, and projection-removal intervention. If no shared alias is detected, diagonal ownership locks the owned concept with the largest simultaneous 95% lower ownership bound for input-induced displacement closure and the same read/write factorisation. Exact ties in either branch are resolved by the registered concept order: Effusion, Atelectasis, Pneumothorax, Cardiomegaly, Mass, then Nodule. If neither confirmatory condition holds, the project switches to a separately registered Qwen Consolidation input-closure gate on a fresh cohort. The immutable runner validates the complete summary and writes this decision to `artifacts/mechanism-route.json`; invalid or internally inconsistent summaries fail closed. Any visual block pattern in this matrix is exploratory and can motivate only a separately registered confirmation. Only the selected confirmatory structure is replicated once on a second architecture. Prompts, concepts, dose, cohort, and statistics are not changed after observing the matrix.

The budget is one A100, at most 4 GPU-hours and 4 wall-clock hours, with no paid API spend. Dispatch is allowed only on a GPU using less than 500 MiB immediately before launch. The immutable payload is `["timeout","--signal=TERM","--kill-after=60","4h","bash","scripts/server/run_qwen_causal_ownership_gate.sh"]` from a clean pushed `main`. The runner stores all 304,800 per-image outcomes, the exact cohort receipt, per-question metadata, completion markers, joint patient-bootstrap draws, and the deterministic mechanism-route artifact. Deterministic internal replay and pinned read-only review precede `PASS`.

## ICLR evidence expansion

The active research objective is to establish when a readable clinical direction supports a semantic intervention. The next sequence separates answer encoding, input-supported intervention geometry, and architecture transfer. Each comparison retains the accepted six-gate observations and adds a separately identified experiment. Codex owns experiment selection and execution; the independent paper task receives accepted findings and chooses their placement.

1. **Answer-encoding crossover.** Does a clinical-direction effect follow the clinical meaning or the answer symbol when the mapping between them changes? This is the next GPU experiment.
2. **Input-supported intervention geometry.** On matched within-patient studies, how much behavioral change is recovered by a natural activation displacement, its probe component, and its complementary component? Establish a paired behavioral opportunity first; preserve co-occurring-label and acquisition-setting balance, and compare matched perturbation magnitudes. Full donor-token replacement is a measurement positive control. Partial recovery and complementary-component effects test the geometry of causal influence. The detailed protocol is locked before new outcomes are inspected.
3. **Architecture transfer.** Replicate the clinically oriented direction-by-question comparisons in LLaVA using the existing consumed-locus activations and fixed-capacity readout. The cross-model unit is an eligible concept-by-encoding comparison under the same controls, rather than a raw probability-effect ranking. A medical-domain model is a subsequent scope extension if an accessible, verified asset is available.

The first analysis also uses the existing ownership per-patient records to report probability versus logit-margin effects, baseline and steered discrimination, label-conditioned responses, and matrix interaction structure. These are descriptive analyses of existing evidence. They identify measurement or correlation explanations for the new experiments and do not replace earlier gate decisions.

### Qwen answer-encoding crossover

The gate `qwen7b-vislast-answer-encoding` holds the Qwen revision, 336-by-336 processor resolution, consumed `model.visual.blocks.31` locus, six capacity-matched clinical normals, and relative-token intervention fixed. It takes the first 200 rows, in their stored order, from accepted ownership run `20260904T125710Z-a3bd883540eb-causal-ownership`. Each row belongs to a distinct patient. This is a paired sensitivity experiment on an existing cohort. All comparisons use these same patients; the experiment is not an independent-population replication.

For each of the six concepts in the accepted order, use three encodings. The standard encoding retains the accepted yes/no prompt. The two code prompts are:

```text
Does this chest radiograph show {finding}? Answer A if the finding is present and B if it is absent. Reply with A or B only.
Does this chest radiograph show {finding}? Answer B if the finding is present and A if it is absent. Reply with A or B only.
```

The fixed findings are `a pleural effusion`, `atelectasis`, `a pneumothorax`, `cardiomegaly`, `a lung mass`, and `a lung nodule`. For A/B, take the maximum logit over each letter's available single-token plain and leading-space spellings. The two token sets must be nonempty and disjoint. The primary score is the finding-present minus finding-absent logit margin. The raw A-minus-B margin is also retained, and the secondary probability is the sigmoid of the clinically oriented margin. The standard encoding uses the accepted yes/no scoring implementation.

Each question and encoding receives one unsteered baseline and 27 directions at alpha `-0.25` and `+0.25`: six clinical normals, 20 seed-0 isotropic random directions shared across questions and encodings, and a coordinate-permutation sham of that question's normal. The sham permutation follows the 20 random draws. This produces 55 configurations per question/encoding and 198,000 image-condition outcomes. All 18 question/encoding blocks are reported.

Before evaluating the test cohort, a preflight checks the accepted forward-path module, an exact alpha-zero no-op and a nonzero downstream-logit response on a validation image, the A/B token identities, and eight unambiguous text-only presence/absence statements under the two code mappings. Every text-only semantic margin must have the correct sign. A failed preflight makes the measurement gate `BLOCKED` before the full experiment.

For each question, assess baseline clinical discrimination separately under both A/B encodings. A question is eligible for the primary interpretation if each encoding has at least ten positive and ten negative patients, at least 1,900 valid draws out of 2,000 patient bootstrap samples, and a one-sided 95% AUROC lower bound above 0.5. Record all questions, their counts, baseline Brier scores, encoding-to-encoding baseline rank correlations, and eligibility. Ineligible comparisons remain descriptive. If no question is eligible, the primary mechanism comparison is inconclusive.

For a patient, clinical direction, and question at alpha `+0.25`, let `a` and `b` be the raw A-minus-B intervention changes under the A-present and B-present mappings respectively. Define the semantic component `s=(a-b)/2` and the answer-token component `t=(a+b)/2`. A meaning-following intervention reverses its raw direction under the mapping exchange and contributes to `s`; a literal-symbol-following intervention contributes to `t`.

The primary endpoint is the difference between the squared Frobenius norms of the mean token and semantic component matrices, `||mean(t)||_F^2 - ||mean(s)||_F^2`, over all six clinical directions and the baseline-eligible questions. Eligibility is fixed from the baseline data for this conditional comparison, and the retained question identities are reported. Joint patient bootstrap draws (2,000, seed `20260907`) give a two-sided 95% interval; it is one aggregate endpoint. The all-question contrast, each component matrix, clinically oriented ownership margins, random/sham effects, the negative-dose response, and baseline-to-steered AUROC/Brier changes are reported descriptively. A positive interval supports dominant token-following response energy on the eligible comparisons; a negative interval supports dominant meaning-following response energy. Neither establishes concept-specific ownership by itself. Nonsignificance is not evidence of encoding invariance.

The budget is one A100, at most four GPU-hours and four wall-clock hours, with no paid API spend. The runner loads the model once, evaluates batches of 16, and records each completed question/encoding block. Launch only on a GPU using less than 500 MiB immediately before dispatch. The immutable payload is `["timeout","--signal=TERM","--kill-after=60","4h","bash","scripts/server/run_qwen_answer_encoding_gate.sh"]`. A separate `preflight` argument runs only the measurement preflight. Internal tests and the pinned read-only registration review precede GPU dispatch; terminal analysis and pinned read-only review precede evidence handoff.

The design is informed by the demonstrated dependence of activation-patching conclusions on metrics and intervention choices ([Zhang and Nanda, ICLR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042)). The subsequent geometry comparison can use train-fitted concept erasure as a registered comparison where appropriate ([Belrose et al., LEACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03819)). Such comparisons answer additional questions while retaining the original probe-normal protocol and results.

## Evaluation and fairness

- Train/test separation is patient-level; validation may select implementation parameters but never report headline evidence.
- Model comparisons are architecture-matched where claimed; unmatched models are descriptive replication.
- Report confidence intervals, repeated random-control distributions, all registered cells, and multiplicity handling before aggregate claims.
- Record opportunity/oracle bounds: probe performance bounds decodable opportunity; calibrated image-space planting and maximum validated intervention response bound attainable behavioural movement.
- Every hard gate receives internal verification followed by the pinned Claude read-only review. Review cannot create evidence.
