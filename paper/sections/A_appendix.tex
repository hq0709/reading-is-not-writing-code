\section{Registered Protocol and Provenance}
\label{app:protocol}

\paragraph{Prompts.}
The Effusion prompt is ``Is there a pleural effusion in this chest radiograph? Answer yes or no.'' The Edema prompt is ``Is there pulmonary edema in this chest radiograph? Answer yes or no.'' The Consolidation prompt substitutes ``consolidation'' in the same template. The ownership matrix likewise uses one fixed yes/no prompt for each of its six named findings. LLaVA uses \texttt{llava-hf/llava-1.5-7b-hf} at revision \texttt{b234b804b114d9e37bb655e11cbbb5f5e971b7a9}. Qwen uses \texttt{Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct} at revision \texttt{cc594898137f460bfe9f0759e9844b3ce807cfb5}, with processor resolution fixed at $336^2$ pixels.

\paragraph{Rows and seeds.}
The deterministic NIH split hashes patient identity. Probe projection and fitting use seed 0. Type-to-label controls use seeds 0--19. Probe intervals use 2,000 patient-cluster draws with seed 20260827. The independent Qwen cohort excludes every patient in the original intervention set, chooses one row per remaining patient with SHA-256 of \texttt{qwen-direction-specificity-row-v1\textbackslash0<row\_id>}, ranks patients with SHA-256 of \texttt{qwen-direction-specificity-patient-v1\textbackslash0<patient\_id>}, and takes the first 400. Its canonical row-list SHA-256 is \texttt{76be1da9659973d44a0898fa69c579afc4649f329ee227b19d1216efaa1c8bda}; supplement bootstraps use seed 20260903. The ownership matrix selects a third label-blind 400-patient cohort with row-list SHA-256 \texttt{e657a9bd1707f13695b02a341047af6f53981295b840376eb0ea5bbebfe2d312} and bootstrap seed 20260904. The Consolidation cohort excludes all 1,000 earlier Qwen rows and hash-selects one positive/negative pair for 50 patients; its pair-list SHA-256 is \texttt{20dcc8cc3aaa8c65a6f7f759a0ff6bf3b0cf4f1b87f4b65cdc6a15a46c6d85e2}, with bootstrap seed 20260905.

\paragraph{Immutable runs.}
The accepted full runs are \texttt{20260902T191411Z-ffd523c464c8-f99e2f39} (LLaVA Effusion), \texttt{20260902T221534Z-110b84618d1b-edema} (LLaVA Edema), \texttt{20260903T000321Z-caaae3ef346d-qwen-full} (Qwen Effusion), \texttt{20260903T103508Z-456c81bad460-7a3edc4b} (Qwen direction specificity), \texttt{20260904T125710Z-a3bd883540eb-causal-ownership} (Qwen ownership), and \texttt{20260904T162317Z-8a55a4c2f6b6-consolidation-closure} (Qwen input closure). Their recorded GPU wall times are 7,022, 1,823, 2,077, 361, 9,211, and 445 seconds, respectively, for 20,939 seconds total on one A100 per run. Downstream gates reuse accepted activations or directions where registered.
