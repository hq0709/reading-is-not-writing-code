\section{Registered Protocol and Provenance}
\label{app:protocol}

\paragraph{Prompts.}
The Effusion prompt is ``Is there a pleural effusion in this chest radiograph? Answer yes or no.'' The Edema prompt is ``Is there pulmonary edema in this chest radiograph? Answer yes or no.'' LLaVA uses \texttt{llava-hf/llava-1.5-7b-hf} at revision \texttt{b234b804b114d9e37bb655e11cbbb5f5e971b7a9}. Qwen uses \texttt{Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct} at revision \texttt{cc594898137f460bfe9f0759e9844b3ce807cfb5}, with processor resolution fixed at $336^2$ pixels.

\paragraph{Rows and seeds.}
The deterministic NIH split hashes patient identity. Probe projection and fitting use seed 0. Type-to-label controls use seeds 0--19. Probe intervals use 2,000 patient-cluster draws with seed 20260827. The independent Qwen cohort excludes every patient in the original intervention set, chooses one row per remaining patient with SHA-256 of \texttt{qwen-direction-specificity-row-v1\textbackslash0<row\_id>}, ranks patients with SHA-256 of \texttt{qwen-direction-specificity-patient-v1\textbackslash0<patient\_id>}, and takes the first 400. The canonical row-list SHA-256 is \texttt{76be1da9659973d44a0898fa69c579afc4649f329ee227b19d1216efaa1c8bda}. Supplement bootstraps use seed 20260903.

\paragraph{Immutable runs.}
The accepted full runs are \texttt{20260902T191411Z-ffd523c464c8-f99e2f39} (LLaVA Effusion), \texttt{20260902T221534Z-110b84618d1b-edema} (LLaVA Edema), \texttt{20260903T000321Z-caaae3ef346d-qwen-full} (Qwen Effusion), and \texttt{20260903T103508Z-456c81bad460-7a3edc4b} (Qwen direction specificity). Their recorded GPU wall times are 7,022, 1,823, 2,077, and 361 seconds, respectively, for 11,283 seconds total on one A100 per run. The latter three reuse accepted upstream activations or direction artifacts where registered.
