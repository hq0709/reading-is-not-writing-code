\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

At the consumed final visual block, all three registered cells make their target label robustly available to a fixed linear readout. The two LLaVA cells stop at the random/sham intervention rung. Qwen Effusion clears that rung in its original cohort and on 400 independent patients, but a fixed Nodule direction remains reliably stronger. Under the registered protocol, robust decodability therefore does not establish direction-specific causal influence. Clinical interpretation of decoded directions requires controls that test specificity at the same representation, dose, rows, and behavioural endpoint.
